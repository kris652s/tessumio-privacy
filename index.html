Tessumio privacy policy

Updated 1 November 2026

About this policy and contact

Tessumio is an offline number-and-logic puzzle game. This policy covers the Tessumio Android app (com.ktims.games.tessumio). For support or privacy questions, contact the developer at ktimsina77@gmail.com.

Information stored for gameplay

The app stores unlocked levels, campaign and daily-challenge puzzle state, tile-clearing and undo history, completed daily-challenge dates, tutorial status, and sound/vibration preferences in its private Android storage. It keeps verified local backup copies and may preserve older or damaged save copies for recovery. This information is used to run the game, resume progress and recover saves.

Collection, sharing and permissions

The production app does not transmit gameplay data or personal information to the developer. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, in-app purchases, cloud game servers or third-party tracking SDKs. It has no Android Internet permission and does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or shared-storage access. Sound and touch feedback use Android system facilities. An app-scoped signature permission used by AndroidX is not permission to read personal data.

Android backup and device transfer

Android may back up or transfer app data, including progress, preferences and save-recovery files, according to device, operating-system, account and backup-provider settings. This may store copies outside the device. These platform-managed services are separate from the game and are not a Tessumio cloud account. The developer does not receive the platform backup copies. Review your device backup settings and your backup provider's policies for available controls.

Retention and deletion

Progress and local recovery files remain in app storage so the game can resume or recover progress; the app does not automatically delete all recovery data when you restart a puzzle. Android Settings offers controls to clear app storage, and uninstalling can remove local data. These actions may permanently remove progress. Platform backups and device-transfer copies are managed separately by Android and the backup provider and may later restore eligible data. There is no developer-held game account or remote gameplay profile to delete.

Optional support emails

If you choose to email ktimsina77@gmail.com, the support mailbox receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. These are used to answer your request and investigate the issue you describe. Email providers also handle the messages under their own policies. The app does not send these emails or attach saves automatically. Do not send passwords or signing keys. Support correspondence may remain in the mailbox until deleted; you may request deletion by contacting the same address, subject to any applicable legal retention obligations.

Security

Android app isolation and device security help protect local saves. Save checksums detect accidental corruption; they are not encryption and do not guarantee recovery. Device and backup-provider security settings govern additional protection. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

The game does not ask players to enter personal information, regardless of age. There are no accounts, ads, chat or analytics in the app. Parents or guardians can use the support email for privacy questions. This description does not assert a particular age rating; the store audience and rating are declared separately.

Reading this policy online

The in-app policy is bundled text and can be read offline without a web request. If you visit an online copy in a browser, the hosting provider receives information needed to serve the page, such as your IP address and basic request details, and may keep security logs. Those website requests are separate from offline gameplay and are governed by the hosting provider's privacy policy.

Separate apps and policy changes

Tessumio uses its own Android application ID. It does not read or import progress from other app installations. This policy may be updated when the app or its data handling changes. The updated date identifies this policy version; questions can be sent to the contact above.
